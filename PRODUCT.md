# Product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Daily desktop note-takers, students, and problem-solvers writing notes, math, and exercises every day on Windows 10/11 and Linux (Wayland & X11) using a stylus/pen tablet or mouse + keyboard (including Vietnamese IME).

## Product Purpose

**Swift note** is a fast, distraction-free daily handwriting and PDF annotation studio built as a personalized Hybrid fork of Xournal++ (C++20/GTK3/Cairo/Poppler core + Rust `swiftnote-rs` modules). Success means zero-compromise handwriting smoothness paired with a fresh, vibrant, youthful yet Apple-grade minimalist interface and effortless single-key shortcuts inspired by Scrble Ink.

## Positioning

Combines 10+ years of battle-tested Xournal++ handwriting, stylus, PDF, and Vietnamese IME stability with a completely reimagined, energetic minimalist interface, intuitive visual shortcut customization, non-destructive Blackboard auto-ink inversion, lossless clipboard image pasting, Close-up Writing Area, and a fast Rust-indexed Note Library—while preserving 100% `.xopp` compatibility.

## Operating Context

- Primary daily usage: opening/creating notes from the Note Library, writing math/study notes smoothly with quick pen slots, switching tools rapidly via single-key shortcuts, erasing with auto-return to pen, pasting crisp screenshots (`Ctrl+V`), annotating multi-page or password-protected PDFs, using Blackboard mode at night, and zooming into fine handwriting with Close-up Writing Area.
- Desktop toolkit: Native GTK3 + Cairo on Windows 10/11 (MSYS2 UCRT64 portable build) and Linux (Wayland & X11).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Must preserve:** 100% Xournal++ core writing latency, stroke accuracy (no post-lift shift, lag, curve bulge, or stray tail), lossless clipboard PNG/DIB paste, multi-page & password PDF stability, native Vietnamese IME text box, `.xopp` compatibility.
- **Must deliver in redesign:**
  - Rebrand everywhere to **`Swift note`** (exact lowercase `n`), including a new distinctive logo/mark and intentional modern typography supporting Vietnamese diacritics cleanly.
  - Vibrant, youthful, fresh, distinctive visual personality while staying minimalist, crisp, and professional (never dull/generic gray).
  - Simple single-key shortcuts like Scrble Ink (`1–8` pen slots, `E` eraser, `L`/`S` lasso/select, `T` text, `R` ruler, `B` blackboard, `Z` close-up, etc., disabled automatically while typing text) plus an **intuitive visual shortcut & preferences customization UI**.
- **Hard anti-goals (binding):**
  - **ZERO Glassmorphism:** No frosted glass, no liquid glass, no backdrop blur, no translucent chrome—100% solid opaque surfaces only.
  - No cluttered legacy Xournal++ menubar or stacked multi-row toolbars.
  - Both Light Mode and Dark Mode must feel equally crafted and crisp.

## Brand Commitments

- **Brand Name:** `Swift note` (exact capitalization: capital `S`, lowercase `n`).
- **Voice & Vibe:** Modern, youthful, energetic, crisp, focused—minimalist Apple-grade craftsmanship with distinctive character rather than sterile corporate gray.
- **Craft Benchmark:** Scrble Ink's immediate simplicity and tactile focus paired with Apple-level precision.

## Product Principles

1. **Writing feel is sacred:** Visual craft serves focus and speed; never introduce input lag, visual noise, or chrome bloat.
2. **Opaque tactile clarity:** Express freshness and youth through deliberate solid surfaces, refined color accents, crisp geometry, and typography—never translucent glass.
3. **Muscle-memory speed:** Every frequent tool and setting is reachable in one click or one memorable keystroke, visually discoverable and cleanly rebindable.
4. **Honest Light, Dark, and Blackboard modes:** Every theme is tuned intentionally with sharp contrast and effortless switching.
